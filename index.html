Oopbuy shopping guide

A shortlist works better
with a checklist.

An Oopbuy product list helps organize discovery. Your comparison notes help you decide whether an individual item suits your needs.

Record the product link, selected variant, measurements, quoted price and questions for the seller before placing an order.

Start browsing ↗

  1. Choose one category and a purpose

    Start with a clear need: everyday shoes, a lightweight jacket, a matching set or a simple accessory. Compare similar items.

  2. Confirm the exact variant and fit

    Check color, size, dimensions and materials. Compare clothing measurements with your own garments and use the product-specific shoe chart.

  3. Review available inspection photos

    Where inspection or QC photos are available, examine visible stitching, print placement, finish and the selected variant. Photos cannot establish authenticity or reveal every defect.

  4. Estimate the full order cost

    Check item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes and currency conversion where applicable. Read return conditions before paying.

Oopbuy QC checklist

What deserves a closer look?

Keep your comparison consistent across listings. Missing information is a reason to ask a question before ordering.

Product comparison checklist for shoes, clothing and accessories
Category Compare first Useful evidence Question to resolve
Shoes Variant, size chart, upper and sole materials Side, heel, sole and size-label photographs Does the sizing information match your foot measurements?
Hoodies & T-shirts Chest, shoulder, length and fabric composition Measurement photos and close-ups of seams or prints Does the measured fit match a garment you already wear?
Jackets & sets Layering room, lining, closures and individual piece sizes Front, back, fastening and measurement photographs Are all pieces and relevant measurements included?
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening and stretch Waistband, seams and length measurements Will the waist and cut suit your intended fit?
Headwear & accessories Dimensions, adjustability, base materials and hardware Closure, surface and scale-reference photographs Are the size and material details sufficiently clear?

QC means quality control. A visual inspection can document visible details; it is not a guarantee of durability, safety or authenticity.

A few practical answers about spreadsheet discovery, product checks and where this guide takes you.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list associated with shopping through Oopbuy. Entries may include product links, categories, prices, sizing notes or photos. Each listing and its current availability should be checked separately.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide that links to CAFANS categories. It does not claim affiliation with Oopbuy or the brands mentioned.

How should I compare products from a spreadsheet?

Compare the exact product variant, measurements, materials, current price, seller information and return conditions. Where available, review recent inspection photos and estimate shipping and applicable taxes before paying.

Do product photos prove authenticity?

No. Photos and brand names do not establish authenticity. Check the seller’s evidence, provenance and applicable buyer protections. Editorial images on this page are not inventory records or authentication evidence.

Does a listed price include international delivery?

Do not assume it does. Check which costs are included and estimate any domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes and currency conversion charges relevant to your order.

Where do the category links lead?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS pages for shoes, hoodies, T-shirts, jackets, pants, headwear, sets, underwear, jerseys and accessories. General browsing links lead to the CAFANS homepage.
